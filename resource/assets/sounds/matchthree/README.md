# Match Three sounds

Five original synthesized glass-marimba cues for the "Be Jewelled" theme, with soft attacks
and short echo tails. Regenerate with `python3 scripts/matchthree-sounds.py`.

`stones/` holds the "Sea Stones" theme's five cues: pebbles clicking together (noise ticks
through short stone resonances) and waves washing over shingle (swelling filtered noise, with
pebbles rattling as the water drains). Regenerate with `python3 scripts/matchthree-stone-sounds.py`.

No sampled or third-party material.
16-bit PCM WAV, mono, 44.1 kHz; all clips fit mobile SoundPool limits.
