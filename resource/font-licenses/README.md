# Bundled fonts

`resource/fonts/` accepts font files only, so the record of where each one came from lives here.

## B612

`resource/fonts/B612-Regular.ttf` draws Sudoku's digits: the board, the pencil marks, the keypad,
and the home-screen tile. B612 was designed for Airbus cockpit displays, where a misread digit
is costly, so every numeral has a shape of its own: the 3 has a flat top and never reads as an
8, the 6 and 9 end in straight open tails, the 1 has a foot the 7 lacks, and the 4 is open.
Only the Regular face ships; Sudoku draws its digits at normal weight.
